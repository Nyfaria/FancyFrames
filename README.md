# Fancy Frames

**Fancy Frames** is a mod that adds new painting and item frame variants to Minecraft! These frames are designed to glam up your rooms, bases, and display areas while seamlessly fitting into Vanilla Minecraft.

There is a frame for each Minecraft tier: wood, stone, copper, gold, iron, diamond, emerald, and netherite.

# How to apply a frame

To obtain a frame, craft it from its ingredient and a vanilla item frame. It’s as easy as placing four of the ingredient around the vanilla item frame! For example, four diamonds around an item frame will give you the diamond frame.

To use: place down a Vanilla item or painting. Then, `shift + right click` the placed item or painting to apply the frame. Simple as that!
